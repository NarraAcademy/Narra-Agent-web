## About Narra Agent

Narra Agent is an AI-powered cryptocurrency investment assistant designed to provide investors with real-time market analysis, intelligent investment insights, and deep blockchain ecosystem intelligence.

### Core Features

- **🤖 AI-Driven Market Analysis**: Leverage advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze crypto market trends
- **📊 Real-Time Data Tracking**: Monitor on-chain data and market dynamics across 50+ mainstream blockchain networks
- **💡 Smart Investment Advice**: Personalized investment strategies based on risk assessment and market sentiment
- **🔍 In-Depth Research Reports**: Professional analysis covering emerging projects, DeFi protocols, and NFT markets
- **⚡ Instant Market Alerts**: Real-time notifications for critical market events and price movements

### Supported Blockchain Ecosystems

Narra Agent covers all major blockchain networks, including but not limited to:

- **Layer 1**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana, Polygon, Avalanche
- **Layer 2**: Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, zkSync
- **DeFi Ecosystem**: Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Aave, Compound
- **Emerging Chains**: Latest blockchain technologies and innovative projects

## How to Use

### 1. Sign Up

Visit [Narra Agent](/) and register using your Google account or email.

### 2. Start Chatting

Enter your questions in the chat box, for example:
- "What important news is there in the crypto market today?"
- "Analyze BTC's market trend"
- "What new projects in the BSC ecosystem are worth watching?"

### 3. Get Professional Analysis

Narra Agent will provide detailed market analysis and investment advice based on real-time data.
